# Recipe Rework

Ever struggled to justify using **Ionized Fuel** as a power source, or wondered why **Leached Ingots** exist when you can make **Pure Ingots** instead?

This mod is here to fix that.

Recipe Rework rebalances selected vanilla recipes and adds a few new ones to create more meaningful choices when designing your factories.

**Don't forget to check out complete recipe list with screenshots below =)**

**Note:** While the mod is fully playable, some recipes may still change based on feedback. My goal is to keep it a vanilla-friendly experience.

### Design Philosophy

The goal is to make alternative recipes more competitive, so that choosing the optimal production strategy depends on your factory, resources, and priorities — rather than one recipe simply outperforming all the others.

The mod rebalances dozens of vanilla recipes to give more of them a meaningful reason to exist, while keeping their advantages and trade-offs relevant.

### What do you get?

- **29 rebalanced recipes** — Adjustments to existing recipes to make different production strategies more viable.
- **6 new recipes** — Additional options that open up interesting alternative production chains.
- **Vanilla-friendly design** — Changes are designed to preserve the original game's progression, logic, and overall feel.

### Feedback & Bug Reports

Balancing recipes is no easy task. There's a lot to consider: production ratios, power and space requirements, how recipes compare to one another, and, above all, variety and fun from the player's perspective. And even though I'm a fairly experienced Satisfactory player, there's no way I can get everything perfectly balanced on my own.

So feel free to share your suggestions or report bugs! Let me know what you think about individual recipes and what you think could be improved, changed, or added.

You can share your feedback:
- on the [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/ivanio-limited/RecipeRework/issues)
- or by messaging me on Discord: `@ivanio_limited`

<h3 align="center"> Instant Scrap </h3>

- **Problem:** The original recipe was too expensive for the little benefit it offered beyond saving space.
- **Solution:** Sulfur now provides more than just extra Scrap per Bauxite: you also get Dissolved Silica, which can be used to produce more Aluminum Ingots.
- **Attention:** You won't get enough Silica to process all the Scrap. However, there's no need to bring in extra Silica. The intended approach is to produce as many Ingots with Silica as possible (86%) and smelt the rest (14%) into Pure Aluminum Ingots. This yields ~1.41 Ingots per Bauxite. Smelting all the Scrap using the default recipe with extra Silica yields 1.48 Ingots per Bauxite, which isn't much of a difference.

<details><summary>P.s.</summary>

If you're used to dumping excess Water into the Wet Concrete Refinery, this recipe chain already requires Limestone to turn Dissolved Silica into solid Silica. So you don't even need to set up an extra line you wouldn't otherwise need.

That said, this isn't the intended design. Feel free to sink it if you wish, but remember:

**FICSIT does not waste!**

</details>

<details><summary>P.s.s.</summary>

The Dissolved Silica recipe (not Quartz Purification) is also unlocked when obtaining this recipe.

<summary align="center"><h2>Rocket Fuel and Ionized Fuel</h2></summary>

- **Analysis:** Let's compare each (Oil-based) fuel type to the next tier in terms of energy per m³ and conversion ratios (using basic recipes).

<div align="center">

| Ingredient → Product | Item (m³) ratio | Energy per m³ ratio |  Effective Energy Multiplier (EEM) |
| :------------------: | :-------------: | :-----------------: | :--------------------------------: |
|     Fuel → Turbo     |      6 : 5      |   750 MJ : 2000 MJ  |  5 × 2000 MJ / (6 × 750 MJ) = 2.22 |
|    Turbo → Rocket    |      6 : 10     |  2000 MJ : 3600 MJ  |  10 × 3600 MJ / (6 × 2000 MJ) = 3  |
|   Rocket → Ionized   |      1 : 1      |  3600 MJ : 5000 MJ  | 1 × 5000 MJ / (1 × 3600 MJ) = 1.39 |

</div>

- **Problem:** On the one hand, Rocket Fuel has by far the highest **EEM**, while Ionized Fuel has the lowest. Rocket Fuel also has the best Somersloopable fuel recipes, especially Nitro Rocket Fuel, giving you the greatest net power increase per Somersloop. The only exception is Dark-Ion Fuel, but its ratios are terrible to begin with, so I wouldn't consider it. The **EEM** is a mathematical way to quantify how much more power you can get (gross) by upgrading to the next fuel tier. That's why Rocket Fuel feels so powerful and desirable: it triples the energy output in exchange for a reasonable amount of Nitrogen Gas. You even get some Compacted Coal back! Meanwhile, Ionized Fuel increases energy output by just 1.39×. And that's before accounting for the power cost of producing Power Shards, which can actually make the net power output **negative**.

- **Solution:** Balanced the EEMs by moderately nerfing Rocket Fuel and substantially buffing Ionized Fuel. Also adjusted the production speeds of both Ionized Fuel recipes to make them more viable for Somersloop boosting.

- **Note:** The mod only changes recipes, not fuel energy values. All burn times remain unchanged, whether in generators, vehicles, jetpacks, or drones.
